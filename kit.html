+---------------------------+
| Pocket Acid Kit Generator |
+---------------------------+




Select your original samples above. If they aren't already, they will
be resampled to 32 kHz. Samples are matched to kit slots based on
filenames.

Files with the following filenames must be provided:

- bd: bass drum
- sd: snare drum
- ch: closed hi-hat
- oh: open hi-hat
- oc: open+closed hi-hat
- lt: low tom
- ht: high tom
- cy: cymbal
- rs: rimshot
- cp: clap

The file extensions don't matter and the file can be of any format
supported by your browser.

You can of course assign whichever function you prefer to a sample
slot, but those are the labels displayed in Pocket Acid. Note the
special behavior of hi-hat sounds. ch, oh and oc will all interrupt
each other, and oc will play if you have enabled both ch and oh on
the same step.

The conversion is performed in the browser, so none of the files will
be uploaded anywhere.

The kits should be placed in the same directory as your save files,
and Pocket Acid will load them on startup. The filenames (except the
file extension) must not exceed 8 characters in length.
